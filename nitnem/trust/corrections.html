How a source correction will be made

1. Preserve: retain the old exact Gurmukhi bytes, SHA-256, occurrence ID, source edition identifier and original surroundings. Do not rewrite the source merely because another website differs.

2. Compare: gather independent named physical or authoritative reference editions. Record the exact passage and original context, and distinguish literal spelling errors from legitimate tradition or edition variants.

3. Review: require a qualified independent Sikh/Punjabi original-text reviewer. Explain any disagreement, spell out which edition we will follow, and clear source licensing before public distribution.

4. Version: when authorized, publish a new immutable source edition with old/new hashes, a dated reason, review identity/role and a rollback route. Do not mutate saved personal reflections, rewrite historical evidence or self-approve a change.

5. Disclose: record whether the issue remains OPEN, was RESOLVED, or is intentionally supported as a named VARIANT. Only independently justified decisions may move from OPEN.

No text changes are being requested or asserted on this page. This is a pending issue register within an owner-only beta, not an edited public Gurbani source.

What the machine checks establish

SHA-256 receipts, source-structure tests and browser navigation checks establish that **stored digital candidate bytes and their on-screen order remain consistent**. They cannot establish correct Gurbani grammar, authentic pronunciation, final religious authority, an approved Gurmukhi manuscript or the rights to republish a modern interpretation.

Qualified editions, attributed interpretations and licensed performance recordings remain separate evidence families. We do not accept an AI-generated verdict as the independent source approval.
