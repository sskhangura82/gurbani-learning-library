About this reading collection

This collection follows the familiar five morning banis, evening Rehras and night Sohila. It includes all 40 pauris of Anand Sahib. Chaupai includes the 25-pauri section and a clearly marked four-pauri continuation; prayer boundaries differ between traditions.

The Gurmukhi uses documented digital editions, with source corrections recorded. English and Punjabi meanings and passage summaries are AI-assisted educational drafts awaiting qualified human review. Earlier detailed Study sections are chiefly in English; the newly completed Jaap sections and Anand Pauri 24 also have extended Punjabi study. The silent word guide is a visual reading aid.

Ardas is a separate accompanying prayer and is not included in these seven readers.
