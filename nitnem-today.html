A note on the text

This is a practical, linked-reading preview. We preserve our complete Japji reader, while the remaining banis open on independent external websites. Those sites own their text and may present different manuscript spellings, recitation boundaries or translations. Their editions have not been approved as the final source for our app.

Our own seven-bani original-text library, offline operation, pronunciations and teaching still require independent source collation, qualified Sikh/Punjabi review and physical iPhone acceptance.
