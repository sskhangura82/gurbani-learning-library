About this tool

About the pace and the research

Studies of slow breathing have measured short-term changes in blood pressure and heart-rate variability, the variation in time between heartbeats. One small study compared breathing at 6 and 15 breaths per minute.[1]

Other research used a personally measured resonance rate. This guide does not measure yours, and six breaths per minute will not suit everyone.[2]

A review found short-term physiological effects across studies; longer-term benefits remained uncertain.[3] Results in study groups do not promise a particular benefit for you.

  1. Joseph and colleagues, 2005 · Controlled breathing study
  2. Steffen and colleagues, 2017 · Individual resonance breathing study
  3. Shao and colleagues, 2024 · Systematic review and meta-analysis

Evidence note prepared for independent review. This tool has not been clinically validated.
