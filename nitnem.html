WHY WE ARE NOT RUSHING THE SOURCE TEXT

A complete reader requires a trustworthy edition.

Japji Sahib has all original occurrences and bilingual explanation drafts. For the other banis, we are recording source, sequence, boundaries, provenance, and review requirements before calling them readable. Until each text has been checked, the site will not invent verses or send a reader to a false edition.
