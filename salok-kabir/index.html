About this collection and its sources

This reader contains the complete sequence of 243 numbered Saloks under the heading “ਸਲੋਕ ਭਗਤ ਕਬੀਰ ਜੀਉ ਕੇ”. The original heading precedes the invocation. Six original Mahalla headings appear before Saloks 209, 210, 211, 214, 220 and 221. Named voices within other Saloks are discussed in their passage studies.

This is one collection within Sri Guru Granth Sahib Ji. Bhagat Kabir Ji’s Bani across the Raags will have its own source-linked catalogue. The following Salok Sheikh Farid collection is separate.

The selected edition keeps all 486 verse records from a pinned OpenGranth source. The opening heading follows the exact alternate reading without a terminal danda, supported by SriGranth and inspected page images. The raw extraction and this choice are recorded separately.

Of the 494 displayed source entries, 493 match the second digital transcription exactly. Salok 177 retains the selected Gurmukhi nasal-mark encoding; the second website uses a private-use glyph. Its equivalence remains unresolved.

Ang labels are locators from the selected digital witness. Printed-page spot checks show differences at Saloks 177 and 231, recorded separately in Study. A misplaced Farid line is excluded from this Kabir sequence with its provenance retained. Complete collation against an authenticated printed edition and qualified human review are pending.
