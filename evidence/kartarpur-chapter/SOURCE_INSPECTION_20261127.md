## Source positions kept separate

The early pauri's seventh original line names Sodar and Aarti and refers to Jap at Amrit Vela. Macauliffe's printed page 181 names Sodar and Sohila in the evening and Japji in the morning. This is a wording difference, not enough by itself to establish a contradiction in historical practice. Neither term is silently replaced; no combined daily schedule is created.

No precise founding or settlement year is asserted. Founding, later return and final residence are different historical propositions. This draft does not decide between them.

## Rights and authorship

The project stores source metadata, original factual claim summaries, an original AI-assisted manuscript and retrieval digests. It does not store modern translations, article bodies, scans, illustrations, audio or copied book layouts. Source metadata carries METADATA_ONLY_NO_TEXT_OR_IMAGES; this is a limit on intake, not a claim that custodians have granted permission.

The English manuscript is explicitly AI-assisted. A qualified human has neither authored nor reviewed it. It cannot enter the existing human-authored chapter slot. Five secondary-source propositions retain UNVERIFIED_ATTRIBUTION because the present registry has no separate scholarly-account class; that conservative choice does not mean the source was unread.

## What a qualified reviewer should decide

1. Whether the English historical descriptions of the original pauri are accurate and sufficiently qualified.
2. Which manuscript or critical/print edition should support the Vaaran citation.
3. Which early sources substantiate household, farming, kitchen and succession details, and whether the claim classifications should change.
4. Whether the later source's composition names require explanation, without harmonising the accounts by assumption.
5. Whether the story is suitable for families after any necessary revision.

No date, rights grant, qualification or independent transmission lineage is inferred from agreement between websites.
