# DRAFT — permission request regarding the daily Hukamnama

**STATUS: DRAFT ONLY. NOT SENT. NO CUSTODIAN HAS BEEN CONTACTED.**
Drafted by Claude at the founder's standing instruction that permission requests may be
drafted but not acted on. Sending, addressing and any correspondence are the founder's act.

I have deliberately **not** researched contact details, since looking up how to reach a
custodian is a step toward contacting them. The founder or ChatGPT can address it
appropriately; I have left the addressee blank.

---

To: [addressee — left blank deliberately]

Respected Sewadars,

I am writing with a request and, first, with thanks for the daily publication of the
Hukamnama from Sri Harmandir Sahib.

I am building a free Gurbani learning application for my family and for anyone who finds it
useful. It will always be free. It carries no advertising, no subscriptions, no accounts, no
tracking and no analytics. Anything a reader writes stays on their own device and is never
transmitted anywhere.

The application shows the original Gurbani text only. It does not reproduce any modern
translation, commentary, recording or page layout, and it presents no explanation as
authoritative. Where an explanation has not been written and reviewed by a qualified person,
the application shows an empty space saying so rather than filling it in.

My request concerns the daily Hukamnama. I would like to show each day's reading, as
published by Sri Harmandir Sahib, with the original Gurmukhi text and a clear statement of
where it comes from. I would not alter the text, would not substitute a different reading if
the day's selection were unavailable, and would not present my own words as the meaning of
the Guru's.

I am aware that the Hukamnama being published publicly does not by itself mean I may
re-publish it, which is why I am asking rather than assuming.

Specifically I would like to ask:

1. Whether you permit an application to show each day's Hukamnama, attributed to Sri
   Harmandir Sahib, using the original Gurmukhi text.
2. Whether there is a form of attribution you would prefer.
3. Whether there is anything you would prefer an application like this **not** to do.
4. Whether there is a person or office I should correspond with for this.

If the answer is no, or not yet, I will simply not include the daily reading, and I would be
grateful to know either way.

With respect and thanks,

[founder's name and contact — left blank deliberately]

## Notes for the founder, not part of the letter

- **The three parts are separable.** The scripture text is centuries old and raises no
  copyright question. The *dated selection* is the item this letter is actually about. Any
  modern explanation or translation is copyrighted and we must not take it — we write our own
  or ship none, and the application currently ships labelled empty slots, which is correct.
- **What the letter deliberately does not do:** it does not ask for audio or recordings, which
  remain a separate and larger rights question and are paused; it does not ask for
  translations or commentary; and it does not claim any existing permission.
- **If the answer is no**, the Daily publisher refuses cleanly — RT-028 verified that an
  unavailable source produces a visible unavailable date and never substitutes another
  reading. Nothing breaks; the feature simply stays off.
- **I have contacted nobody and will not.**
