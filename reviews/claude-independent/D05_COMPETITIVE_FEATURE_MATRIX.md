## Reading the matrix honestly

- The right-hand columns are mostly K. They show where to look, not what is true today.
- Where this app is alone: no account with private notes on device; a calm silent guide;
  no engagement mechanics.
- Where it is plainly behind others on the evidence I do hold (D and H cells): number of
  compositions, choice of bani length, search, named sources with evidence, human-made
  word-level study, and any licence at all.
- A claim of "best" would need the same task list run by people on real devices against
  each product. That has not been done by anyone.
