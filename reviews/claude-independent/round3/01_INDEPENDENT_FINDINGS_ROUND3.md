NEEDS_REVISION on nine points before anything is built on it.

What matters most holds. All 19 lines are exact, each Salok is whole, nothing of a neighbour is
included, the page refuses damaged data every time, it stores nothing and contacts no one, and it
tells the reader plainly that this is a draft and not the whole of Sri Guru Granth Sahib Ji.

fingerprint as the lessons, are the two repairs that should come before any growth.

repair candidate (#40) waits with no new commit. My view has not changed: the next commit should be
to the repair.
