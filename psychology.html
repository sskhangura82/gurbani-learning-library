FROM HISTORICAL THEORIES TO EVIDENCE

The psychology we bring into the conversation.

Freud's questions about motives remain historically influential, but different ideas have very different empirical support. We do not rank human spiritual worth by personality traits or use psychological labels to explain every Gurbani passage.

A DIFFERENCE WORTH PRESERVING

Two ways of asking, not one answer.

Gurbani speaks within a Sikh theological and ethical tradition. Psychology investigates patterns of mind, behaviour and relationships using theories and empirical methods. Their comparison can illuminate a reader's lived questions without making either a substitute for the other.
