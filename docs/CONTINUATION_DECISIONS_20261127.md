- Keep the existing Gurbani learning application and its reading experiences.
- Add Sri Dasam Granth as a distinct Sikh collection, with its own source,
  composition and edition identities. Existing Jaap, Savaiye and Chaupai reader
  destinations remain the same. Three selected readings are not the full Granth.

  and the trusted people to whom he gives access.

  family's existing reader reflections do not acquire an account requirement.

  or private Git repository alone is not content access control.

  revoking already downloaded/decrypted material are not provided by this static
  design. Never place the passphrase in Git, a URL, a workflow or a screenshot.
- Existing public research references have historical public copies. Removing
  a reference from the current application does not erase those copies.

## History

ChatGPT prepares discrete claims with authentic, identifiable sources and precise
locators. Actual Claude independently checks that the source supports the exact
wording and qualifications. Both reviews bind the material actually inspected.
Changing that material requires a new check. A GPT worker is not Claude.

The result is source-support review, not a certificate of historical truth.
Tradition, early accounts, scholarly reconstruction and disputed assertions keep
their distinct labels. Qualified-human review is truthfully recorded when absent
and is not reintroduced as an automatic blocker contrary to this instruction.
