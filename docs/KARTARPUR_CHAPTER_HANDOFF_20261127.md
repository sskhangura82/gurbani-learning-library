## Findings for the reviewer

The early account names Sodar/Aarti and Jap; the later Macauliffe account names Sodar/Sohila and Japji. Both are preserved with their own locators. This is not presented as a proved contradiction.

A modern institutional account supplies family, farming/kitchen and succession details. These are attributed in the prose, and their underlying early-source support remains a review task. We do not invent an independent lineage for a website, a founding year, a daily routine, conversations or motives.

The Encyclopaedia's old access statement is not current travel evidence. The SGPC page could not be read directly; its search summary is not used as proof. Macauliffe's printed page markers were inspected in a transcription, not a physical volume.
