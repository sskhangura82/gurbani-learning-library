## 1. Design verdict: **CONDITIONAL PASS**

The interaction architecture is a real improvement over R7 and the honesty posture is unusually disciplined. Two user-facing honesty defects (D1, D2) and two consistency defects (D3, D4) are material and should be resolved before founder acceptance. None is severe enough to fail the design work itself.

### Material defects

**D1 — Stale version string in the user-facing provenance panel. Severity: MEDIUM. Source-code inference.**
`js/japji-full.js:181` — the *Sources & actual coverage* panel, reachable from the end-note and from Explore, ends with the literal string `0.9-japji-v1-r6 · public family preview.` The same build declares `GLP-001 · 0.9-japji-v1 · M0 candidate 1` in the footer (`full-japji.html:19`), `0.9-japji-v1-m0-candidate-1` in `GLP_CONTINUOUS_DEBUG` (`js/japji-full.js:236`), and `"runtime": "0.9-japji-v1-m0-candidate-1"` in `docs/M0_APP_MANIFEST.json`. The one panel whose stated purpose is telling the family what they are reading and how far it has been reviewed reports a version two releases stale, and contradicts the footer on the same page. This appears inherited through R7 rather than introduced by M0, but it ships in this candidate. Not covered by any check in `evidence/m0-browser.json` or `evidence/m0-portable.json`.

**D2 — Draft disclosure is visually suppressed exactly where long reading happens. Severity: MEDIUM. Source-code inference.**
Three mechanisms compose badly:
- `js/japji-full.js:114` unhides `#coverage-note` ("All teaching is a draft awaiting qualified human review…") whenever mode ≠ read.
- `css/japji-full.css:78` clips that same note to screen-reader-only under `body.quiet-reading` (immersive).
- `css/japji-full.css:54` hides `.expand-hint` ("Draft meaning · tap to explore") on every scripture line after the first in a passage.
- `js/japji-full.js:220` enters immersive **automatically** once `scrollY > 320`, unless `persistentControls` is set; `js/japji-full.js:152` also enters it on Play.

Net effect for a sighted reader in Understand mode: scroll past the first screen and the visible draft banner disappears, while the per-line draft hint is already suppressed below line 1. Bilingual meanings then read as settled text with no visible provenance. The disclosure survives in the accessibility tree (clipped, not `display:none`), so this is a visual-honesty regression, not a total loss. `evidence/m0-day-understand-390.jpg` and `evidence/m0-amrit-understand-390.jpg` both show the "Draft meaning · tap to explore" hint on the first occurrence only, consistent with this reading.

**D3 — "Meaning" versus "Understand" vocabulary split. Severity: MEDIUM. Inspected evidence + source.**
The dock button is visibly labelled **Meaning** (`full-japji.html:26`; visible in `m0-grouped-menu-390.jpg`, `m0-day-read-390.jpg`, `m0-day-understand-390.jpg`, `m0-amrit-understand-390.jpg`, `m0-study-390.jpg`, `m0-large-320.jpg`). The certificate, the work-order framing, the QA frame buttons, and `evidence/m0-browser.json` ("Read to Understand preserves occurrence, offset and guide") all call the mode **Understand**. The accessible name carries both (`aria-label="Meaning · Understand in English and Punjabi"`), which is why the check *"Depth controls include their visible English label in the accessible name"* passes without exposing the split. Compounding it, "meaning" is overloaded across three distinct objects: the dock mode, the per-line `.simple-meaning` draft, and the Study panel's "Passage meaning" heading. The certificate's claim of "unboxed Read/Understand" describes a control no user sees by that name.

**D4 — Pace drill-down has no return path; Appearance does. Severity: MEDIUM. Source-code inference.**
`Appearance` renders inside the same dialog and opens with an explicit `← Reading menu` button (`js/japji-full.js:172`). `Pace` instead closes the Reading menu entirely and opens the separate `#pace-tray` (`action('reader-pace') → closeDialog(openPaceTray)`, `js/japji-full.js:193`, `:79–84`). On close, focus goes to `#tools-open` — the dock, not the menu the user came from. Two sibling entries in the same 2×2 grid therefore unwind to different places. The certificate's claim that Escape "restores the source anchor and focus to the visible dock" is accurate and is confirmed by the browser check *"Closing pace returns keyboard focus to visible dock"*; the defect is that returning to the dock is the wrong destination for a second-level item reached from a menu. No check covers a Pace→menu return, whereas one exists for Appearance.

### Requested assessment areas

- **Scripture dominance — holds.** In Read, only `.tuk` renders; meanings, deep-study and unit lessons are `display:none`, and section captions are clipped (`css/japji-full.css:67`, `:15–16`). `m0-day-read-390.jpg` and `m0-large-320.jpg` show an uninterrupted Gurmukhi column. In Understand (`m0-day-understand-390.jpg`) scripture at 30px dominates ~14–16px drafts. Study (`m0-study-390.jpg`) replaces the column with the lesson — inherent to the mode, not a dominance failure.
- **Unboxed Read/Understand — holds.** `.tuk` carries `background:none!important`, no border, no radius (`css/japji-full.css:71`). `.simple-meaning` is `border:0;background:transparent` (`:14`) with only a hover wash. The sole chrome is a 2px left accent rule on the current tuk (`:72`), visible in `m0-large-320.jpg`. No cards, no rules between pauris; check *"Accessible pauri headings retained without visible rules or cards"* PASS.
- **Grouped menu density — materially improved, and the comparison is fair.** `m0-before-r7-menu-320.jpg` shows ten-plus stacked full-width buttons with an active scrollbar, content cut mid-label at "Explore · learning, raag & gurdwaras". `m0-grouped-menu-390.jpg` shows a 2×2 grid of four essentials, a collapsed disclosure, and a two-button footer. Measured dialog height 473.78px (`evidence/m0-browser.json`) against an 844px viewport. The two screenshots are at different widths (320 vs 390), so the comparison demonstrates the structural change, not a like-for-like measurement.
- **54px dock with six 44px targets — structurally sound, not merely asserted.** `css/japji-full.css:81–91`: `4px` top/bottom padding + 44px children + 1px borders = 54px. Six targets: `#play` and `#tools-open` at `flex:0 0 44px`; `#dock-position` at `flex:1 1 44px; min-width:44px`; `.modes` at `flex:0 0 132px` containing three buttons at exactly `44px` each. - **Narrow current location — present, legibility unverified.** See D5. Visible at 320px in `m0-large-320.jpg`; `title` and `aria-label` carry the untruncated string (`js/japji-full.js:124–125`).
- **Accessible reveal — adequate.** `#tools-open` carries `aria-haspopup="dialog"`, `aria-controls`, and a maintained `aria-expanded` (`full-japji.html:29`, `js/japji-full.js:164`, `:167`). Escape exits immersive and returns focus to the dock (`:197`). Native `<dialog>.showModal()` for both the menu and the pace tray. Weakened by D6.
- **Depth transitions — holds.** `changeMode` captures `anchor()` before and `restoreAnchor()` after (`js/japji-full.js:160`); Study pauses the guide with an honest status ("Study mode: the visual guide is paused. External audio is unchanged."). `evidence/m0-browser.json` records sub-pixel anchor retention across depth change at rest, during Play, and at deep anchors 30/208/378.
- **60px (200%) wrapping — holds, as a deliberate trade.** `css/japji-full.css:124` changes `.tuk-ending` from `white-space:nowrap` to `display:inline-block; white-space:normal`, so the pair stays atomic where it fits and breaks internally rather than overflowing. All 385 endings stay on the preceding word's row at 30px and 48px across 320/390, and all 385 occurrences wrap inside an unclipped 1.9 line box at 200% with `violations: []`. `m0-large-320.jpg` shows the consequence honestly: single-word orphan rows ("ਜਾਣੈ"). That is the correct trade at 200% on a 320px column, and the settings copy discloses it.

## 2. Release verdict: **BLOCKED for merge and publication**

This is independent of the design verdict above. A visual design pass does not and cannot close a device or lifecycle gate.

Three gates are **NOT RUN**, self-declared in both the certificate and the evidence:

1. **Actual browser R7 → M0 → R7 → M0 lifecycle — NOT RUN.** Four versioned loopback bundles were built; none was executed in a controlled loopback browser. `evidence/m0-browser.json` records `secureContext:false`, `serviceWorkerAPI:false`, `webLocks:false` — the available preview is insecure HTTP and cannot register a service worker or acquire Web Locks under any configuration. The worker and locking results in `evidence/m0-portable.json` are labelled by the file itself as "Node portable tests; Web Locks and service worker are simulations." A prepared fixture is not a pass.
2. **Real-browser saved-learning write / reload / upgrade — NOT RUN.** The insecure preview refuses writes without Web Locks. Portable export/import, stale-tab and concurrency contracts pass as simulations only. No claim of real-browser persistence is supported.
3. **Physical iPhone Safari / Home Screen / VoiceOver / Dynamic Type / airplane mode — NOT RUN.** No device was available. All seven screenshots are desktop Chrome viewport approximations at CSS widths, not Safari, and establish nothing about iOS text rendering, safe-area geometry, OS-level 200% Dynamic Type, global browser zoom, VoiceOver, system reduced-motion, or a sustained reading session.

Additionally open, and not closeable by this review: **Pauri 13 line 1** (independent source comparison), and **qualified religious / translation review** of all bilingual meanings and the 42 Study drafts. The candidate's own coverage panel discloses the collation gap as "384 matches … and one corrupted comparison line; the frozen scripture was not altered" without naming the line (minor; LOW).

I also note the two deliberately updated source contracts (R6 pace slider, R7 numeric shortcut in the Reading menu). The certificate discloses these as changed rather than reporting them as unchanged assertions, which is the correct handling; I did not re-derive their prior content.

**Recommendation.** Keep the PR draft and unmerged. Resolve D1 and D2 before founder acceptance — both are honesty-surface defects in a project whose governance rests on honesty, and both are small, local fixes. D3 and D4 are worth resolving in the same pass. D5 and D10 should be added to the physical-device matrix rather than fixed blind. Nothing in this review authorises merge, deployment, Pages changes, or replacing the family release; R7 stays live.
