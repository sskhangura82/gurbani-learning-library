# GLP-001 — Psychology Room: interpretive method and limits

**2026-10-24. Pilot content only; no approved therapy, diagnosis, personal assessment or religious endorsement.**

## Mission

The room should help a reader make thoughtful connections between the **original Sikh theological text** and separately sourced psychological ideas, from historically influential theories such as Freud through modern empirically studied personality traits and treatment models.

**The rule:** Original Gurbani is never paraphrased into a clinical doctrine. A psychological theory is never elevated into revelation. The middle column, “Possible connection,” is our educational commentary and must be identified as such.

| Layer | What we may say | What we must not claim |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Gurbani | Exact Unicode Gurmukhi from frozen source; Ang, author and surrounding context. | A made-up verse, an isolated phrase as complete proof, or direct clinical treatment directions. |
| Sikh exegesis | Named sources explain how Nirbhau, Nirvair, Hukam, haumai, craving and attention are interpreted. | AI agreement counts as qualified theological review. |
| Psychology | Describe a named thinker/model, its timeline, evidence and limitations. | Freud equals a modern validated diagnosis, the Big Five measure moral worth, attachment style is destiny or psychological treatment proves spiritual claims. |
| Reflection | A concrete human situation where multiple lenses provoke a better question. | Label someone with a disorder, tell them to ignore danger, or prescribe against professional medical care. |

## Source-backed historical spectrum

- **Freud, classical psychoanalysis:** The unconscious, conflict, id/ego/superego and defense mechanisms deeply influenced psychology's history; many classical claims lack reliable falsifiable predictions or experimental support. See APA Dictionary of Psychology, https://dictionary.apa.org/psychoanalysis; balanced empirical critique https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK623739/ . This does not make every contemporary psychodynamic treatment ineffective.
- **Attachment, Bowlby and Ainsworth:** Importance of early attachment and relational patterns; these constructs are neither permanent categories of individual character nor diagnoses. https://dictionary.apa.org/attachment-theory
- **Beck and cognitive therapy:** Appraisals and automatic thoughts can shape emotional reactions, with testable reframing and evidence-based interventions. https://dictionary.apa.org/cognitive-therapy ; https://dictionary.apa.org/automatic-thoughts
- **Big Five and contemporary personality:** Five descriptive dimensions rather than fixed virtues/vices or religious types. https://dictionary.apa.org/big-five-personality-model
- **Acceptance and Commitment Therapy:** Present-moment awareness, acceptance, values and committed action; 2024 systematic/meta-analytic review supports flexibility-related mechanisms but has important design/sample limitations. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38615492/

## Pilot 1 — Fearlessness without hostility

Exact scriptural anchor: **mool.line01** at Ang 1 contains **ਨਿਰਭਉ ਨਿਰਵੈਰੁ** as descriptions of the Divine.

A short lesson has four accountable parts:

1. Original source with exact hash and scope.
2. Sikh commentary from SikhRI and Guru Nanak Nishkam Sewak Jatha, including the link between fearlessness and non-enmity.
3. Psychology as a distinct lens: threat appraisal can elicit fear; a values-based action need not be aggression; boundaries may be firm without humiliation.
4. A reader-pulled, unscored reflection: “What could I say firmly without dehumanizing another person?”

This does **not** mean Guru Nanak Dev Ji directly taught cognitive therapy, that a human should never feel afraid, or that Nirvair means allowing abuse or injustice.

## Pilot 2 — Haumai, the sense of 'I'

Exact source: Pauri 2 concluding line. A contemporary psychological “ego” is not a literal one-to-one translation of haumai. Freud's ego/id/superego can be studied as historical theories. Cognitive appraisals and self-story concepts are separate scientific lenses; do not diagnose people as “narcissistic” based on a Gurbani reflection.

## Pilot 3 — Craving, reward and values

Pauri 1 contains a line about hunger/craving. A brief psychological discussion may consider reward, habits and goal-directed behavior, but must not shame real hunger, ambition or people dealing with mental-health or substance-use concerns. Without direct source support, an ethical application remains ours.

## Pilot 4 — Silence versus attention

Pauri 1 asks why prolonged outward silence does not automatically yield inner stillness. This may invite comparison to attention and mindfulness research but must not turn Naam Simran into a generic stress-reduction technique or require specific breathing exercises.

## Acceptance and future expansion

- Every psychology topic has sourceId + exact source phrase verified against the current 385-line corpus, a full surrounding original Gurmukhi line visible, descriptive source links and an explicit **draft** distinction.
- At least one qualified Punjabi/Gurbani expert and one evidence-literate psychology/mental-health reviewer review substantive lesson revisions before they are presented as finalized teaching.
- No patient assessment, personality scoring, mental-health screening, clinical advice, AI diagnosis, advice to tolerate abuse, personal note sharing or telemetry.
- The room does not capture personal reflections. Any future personal journal must be an explicit user-consented, browser-local feature with export and delete controls.
- A given connection is a **contextual parallel**, not a conflation. Every clinical research statement includes study quality and limitations; do not treat Freud, Big Five, ACT and attachment models as interchangeable scientific evidence.
- The room is a doorway from the library home to optional educational reflection; do not mix its examples into the immutable original scripture or the source translation layer.
- Any standalone psychology product or clinical feature beyond these educational prompts requires a new founder decision, specialist review and privacy/security risk assessment.

**Future topics to investigate, not approved contents:** grief and meaning; emotion and moral conduct; self-concept and interpersonal boundaries; personality across cultures; life-span development; resilience and service. Never force a Gurbani equivalence to make a modern psychological idea look ancient.
