**Psychology Room.** An optional, separate `psychology.html` site with a subdued sage palette and four original educational draft threads: courage without hostility (Mool Mantar Nirbhau/Nirvair), haumai/self-narrative, craving and attention. Each displays the exact frozen Gurmukhi source line, simple Punjabi explanation, source-linked Sikh discussion, a **separate** psychology lens and an unscored teach-back question. Historical section covers Freud, attachment, Beck/cognitive therapy, Big Five and ACT with explicit limitations. **No psychometric tests, diagnosis, therapy advice, synthetic voice, user data storage, video or analytics.** Scientific/clinical and Punjabi/Sikh specialist reviews remain open.

This material is **research and editorial draft only**, not yet the live Study layer, and contains no replacement Gurmukhi transcription. Mool Mantar receives a separate nine-theme draft manuscript emphasizing fearless courage **without hostility**, truthful boundaries and clearly separated psychology reflection. Pauri 1–2 deeper manuscripts remain in the separate [PR #9](https://github.com/sskhangura82/gurbani-learning-library/pull/9).
