## Research tasks, broken down

**GUR-R1: Place-by-place institutional accuracy.** For **Sri Harmandir Sahib in Amritsar**, **Gurdwara Sahib Fremont**, and **Sikh Gurdwara Sahib San Jose**, independently verify official English and appropriate Gurmukhi names, real public addresses and visitor context, basic institutional history (founding, major phases, architectural significance only as supported), distinctive forms of community service/langar/seva where factually sourced, and appropriate etiquette for a nonmember learner. Document **official evidence URL**, date source published or checked, exact support, unresolved claims, and cultural/religious reviewer. Do not invent history or call a gurdwara approved of our application.

**GUR-R2: Genuine broadcast routes and player restrictions.** For every institution, verify the currently public official destination(s)—audio radio, YouTube channel, official service page—whether a stream is actually live at the time checked, whether embed is technically/permissibly supported, relevant YouTube/player requirements, user-gesture playback, visible minimum size, ads/branding/overlay integrity, no hidden stream audio extraction, and fallback if unavailable. Distinguish “official **channel** link” from “confirmed live **stream**.” Handle clocks as accurately localized IANA time independent of service programme. Search for provider restrictions and any external-site return friction on iPhone, but do not automate playback.

**GUR-R3: Photography and documentary authenticity.** Create an asset/rights ledger per place: proposed photo, original photographer/owner, exact permission or license, permitted alterations and crop, attribution, derivative/redistribution constraints, permission to embed in a family-shareable **public URL**, backup plan and accessibility alt text. Photo on an official website is not automatically licensed for reuse. Founder-shot Fremont/San Jose photos are optional after appropriate permission to photograph; no one is required to provide personal photos. If no real rights-cleared image exists, recommend typography/abstract light rather than AI-generated pictures misrepresented as actual architecture.

**GUR-R4: Meaning of arrival.** Interview the source record—not individuals without permission—to understand each Gurdwara as a place rather than another content tile. Recommend at most **two or three concise sourced facts**, one line about why the place matters, one short “arrive” etiquette step if appropriate and supportable, and one intentional Enter action. Avoid exoticizing Sikh symbols, stereotypes, random devotional background tracks, religious icon clutter, or false claims that a user “visited” in person.

**GUR-R5: Scaling to global sangat.** Define a scalable source/rights record that could eventually support other international Gurdwaras. Required fields: stable `placeId`, official approved display name with language review status, public address/IANA timezone and official source, short sourced history/citations, rights-cleared photo(s) or neutral fallback, verified outbound channel/stream link type, checked date, failover status, provider restrictions, known programme if officially sourced, source steward, review status and change-detection/expiry policy. Be very clear about which data can be verified automatically and which require human confirmation. Do not claim live audio availability from opening hours.

**GUR-R6: Stakeholders and consent boundaries.** Specify which review questions are for the founder, which can be researched online, and which would actually require contacting each Gurdwara or obtaining photo/broadcast/branding permission. **Do not make contacts** under this research directive; prepare respectful templates for optional founder-approved later outreach.
