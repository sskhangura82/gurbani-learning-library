## Research tasks: split them as independent deliverables

**PSY-R1: What kinds of psychology are actually useful?** Create an evidence ladder and historical map: Freud/psychoanalytic concepts, Anna Freud/defenses, Jung if useful (historical caution), Bowlby/Ainsworth attachment, Aaron Beck/CBT, personality Big Five and criticisms of typologies such as MBTI, emotion regulation, contemporary ACT, relationships and values. For each, report historical impact, falsifiability, contemporary strength of evidence, known limitations, cultural validity (including Punjabi/Sikh diaspora if real sources exist), learner-appropriate examples and whether it belongs in v1 or later. Do not use “Freud to today” as a claim Freud's theories are all clinically established.

**PSY-R2: Gurbani-to-psychology crosswalk, not equation.** For each of the four pilot topics, build a table: exact occurrence ID / relevant enclosing Gurbani context / Punjabi and English close reading / named Sikh scholar/commentator interpretation / specific psychological concept and its empirical evidence / where the comparison helps / **where it breaks** / potential harm from conflation / review role/status. Explore Nirbhau/Nirvair with safety, protest and human boundaries; Hukam/haumai with self and agency but **not** Freud's ego as a literal translation; craving and reward without shaming bodily hunger; silence and attention without imposing a breath method as Sikh doctrine. Identify at least four new candidate topics, but rank them and park weak ones.

**PSY-R3: Educational methodology.** Recommend whether to use thematic story, comparative reading, case-based example, self-reflection, teach-back, Socratic questions, lesson progression or optional annotated diagrams. The target is *understanding*, not passing a personality quiz. Differentiate older Punjabi reader, English-speaking newcomer, teenager and experienced Gurbani student without making claims about individual traits. Propose two exemplary complete lessons, **Nirbhau/Nirvair** and **haumai**, in natural bilingual draft prose, with exact original source citations and two explicit alternative readings where substantiated. Authors can review, not publish, until qualified seal.

**PSY-R4: Safeguards.** Prohibit diagnostic tests, automated clinical labels, mental-health treatment, unsupported clinical advice, therapy substitution, implied judgments of readers or relatives, user behavioral profiling, streaks/scores, manipulative engagement and spiritual attainment metrics. Explain when an example might be distressing or shaming and how to handle that gently. No data sent to third parties or new storage without separate approval. State whether psychological facts are historical, current scientific consensus, contested, anecdotal or merely a product analogy.

**PSY-R5: Source quality and reviewer roles.** Name proper primary sources: original Gurbani, Guru Granth Darpan / source scholarship, SikhRI/GNNSJ only where relevant, APA or equivalent professional definitions, actual systematic reviews/meta-analyses and reputable scientific journals. Include date checked, publication date, direct link, evidence type and limits. Specify qualified Sikh/Gurbani/Punjabi review distinct from psychologist/mental-health specialist review and founder acceptance; no AI-only approval.
