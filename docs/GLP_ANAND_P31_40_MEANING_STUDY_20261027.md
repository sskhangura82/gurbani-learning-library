## The closing Anand teachings — brief content/meaning audit

- Pauri 31: spiritual capital/merchant and daily remembrance — not real financial profit or selling Naam.
- Pauri 32: rasna, restless tastes and Hari ras — not a diet, medical drink or prescription to avoid nourishment.
- Pauri 33: body and spiritual Divine light — not actual photons, pregnancy physiology or a judgment of disability.
- Pauri 34: joyful arrival of Divine Beloved, Guru's Shabad and anhad sound — no guarantee that sorrow/depression cannot occur.
- Pauri 35: body, karm and responsible conduct — no claim that disabled or ill people are morally failed.
- Pauri 36: eyes and Divine vision — spiritual seeing, no guarantee of curing blindness or lower worth for blind readers.
- Pauri 37: ears, attentive listening and true Bani — deaf readers equally entitled to accessible Gurbani, no hearing-cure claim.
- Pauri 38: body-cave, breath as an instrument, nine visible and tenth hidden doorway — requires named Sikh theological review, not modern anatomy, breath-holding instructions or a technique promised to raise consciousness.
- Pauri 39: “sacha Sohila” as a song of Truth in Anand Sahib — not the same work as the **separate five-shabad Kirtan Sohila** night bani.
- Pauri 40: the blessing of Anand, bliss and relief from *dookh rog santaap* — faithfully presents what the source says, **not** clinical proof of cure for physical disease, disability or psychiatric distress; usual medical care remains appropriate.

All prose is humanly reviewable **assistant-authored educational drafting**, not a canonical literal translation, certified pronunciation, approved theological interpretation or advice to use devotional recitation instead of healthcare.
