# GLP-001 — Source-first Gurbani learning platform, expansion specification v1
Date: 25 November 2026  
State: **NONPUBLISHING DESIGN AND WORKING PILOT**. Primary reference is the existing public Gurbani Learning Library. This work does not approve any scripture or translation, merge PR #40, or authorize new live AI.

## The product to build

Make Sri Guru Granth Sahib Ji approachable to a first-time learner, serious enough for experienced Punjabi readers, and truthful about uncertainty. Reading comes first; education adds progressively more context. It is one free, private, multi-device library, not separate apps for each bani.

Three entryways:

1. **Known passage** — SGGS > Ang / Raag / author / Shabad / line, or Sukhmani > Ashtpadi > Salok / Pauri. An explicit source identifier, enclosing context and reader link accompany each result.
2. **Question or concept** — search the curated, review-tagged learning index for Naam, Hukam, Haumai, suffering, compassion, birth/death, truthful living, and so on. Search results say why they are relevant. If content is not yet written, say so; never make up a line or verdict.
3. **Surprise me** — choose randomly **among complete, source-bound educational units**, not among isolated verses with no surrounding passage. Reader controls may keep the user at that passage without generating activity scores or streaks.

The first proof of concept is **Sukhmani Ashtpadi 17, SGGS Ang 285–286**: introductory Salok, eight Pauris, bilingual newcomer explanations, substantive deeper reading, vocabulary, optional comparisons and questions. The original scripture text is provided by the **existing** Sukhmani reader. The sample is an editable *educational draft*, not an approved translation or full SGGS search.

## Seven teaching layers and their boundaries

| Layer | Content | Admission and review rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 — Original | Immutable Gurmukhi occurrence, exact source edition, Ang, author, raag, shabad and order | Named source/edition, independent collation and exact hash; never generated by an LLM |
| 2 — Language | Approximate transliteration, morphology, grammar, Punjabi/Braj/Persian/Sanskrit vocabulary, idioms | Separate from original, no claim of certified pronunciation |
| 3 — Meaning | Faithful literal reading and distinct contextual explanation in modern Punjabi and English | AI draft label and named human reviewer on eventual acceptance |
| 4 — Deep study | Full stanza argument, source context, theology, interpretive variations, questions and teach-back | Named scholarship, reasons and uncertainty; not a mass-produced generic moral |
| 5 — Gurbani cross-references | Other SGGS occurrences with Ang, enclosing shabad, reason for connection and limits | Full context before concept similarity; repeated words alone insufficient |
| 6 — Comparative traditions | Buddhism, specific Hindu schools/texts, Christianity, Islam and others **as separate optional lenses** | Primary citations in each tradition; state overlap **and** difference; never claim they teach one doctrine |
| 7 — Modern psychology | ACT, cognitive appraisal, compassion, attention and other evidence-backed constructs where genuinely applicable | Link named research, explain study limitations; do not present Gurbani as clinical treatment or prove theology with clinical outcomes |

Distinguish *word-by-word literal explanation*, *interpretive transcreation*, *historical hypothesis*, *cross-tradition analogy*, *modern psychological model*, and *reflection*. No screen may collapse those into one undifferentiated “meaning.”

### Comparative content contract

Each comparison record must supply:
- exact Sikh passage and its enclosing source range;
- named counterpart, its tradition and primary chapter/verse or peer-reviewed study;
- **similarity** with an explicit reason;
- **difference** with an explicit reason;
- a question encouraging the reader to test whether the comparison is sound;
- contributor, date, review stage and rights for any reproduced content.

Never say “Sikhism is Buddhism,” “Naam is mindfulness,” “Hukam is acceptance therapy,” “Sat equals the Gita's Atman,” or “this line proves a theory of physics.” These are distinct questions, not automatic conclusions. Do not present a modern psychological explanation as Guru Sahib's intended original wording.

### Specific historical trap in Ashtpadi 17

Later sakhis connect the Salok's recurrence with Baba Sri Chand Ji and Guru Arjan Dev Ji. Traditional accounts differ; their historicity is disputed in scholarly work. Sukhmani's own Salok does not name Sri Chand as author. Display this as a **historical-source discussion** apart from original Gurbani. Preserve the precise Sukhmani **ਭਿ** and Japji **ਭੀ** forms rather than silently harmonizing them.

## Long-term source and teaching data model

Maintain a canonical source register of units, not a flat multilingual string:
```text
scripture/composition/occurrence
  exactGurmukhi, occurrenceId, sourceVersion, sha256, Ang, raag, author,
  composition, shabadId, salok, pauri, linePosition, preceding/following context

editorial/unit
  sourceOccurrenceIds[], sourceHashes[], language=en|pa, kind=literal|contextual,
  author, date, editor, status=draft|source_checked|qualified_human_approved

lesson/unit
  fullEnclosingUnit, words[], grammar[], mainArgument, alternatives[],
  comparativeEdges[], reflections[], teachBack[], citations[], reviewStatus

comparison/edge
  fromSourceRange, toTraditionTextOrStudy, similarBecause, differentBecause,
  editorialRisk, bibliography[], reviewer, reviewStatus

learning/local
  currentRead, bookmarks, optional learner reflections, language/appearance
```
Keep all learning saved in the user's device until they explicitly export or transfer it. Never place reflections in the repository or cloud logs. Preserve old line identifiers with a documented migration and recovery route.

A future question engine must be *retrieval-first*: cite the exact source lines, separate what is written from what is inferred, show the entire shabad/pauri context, ask for context when ambiguous, and say “not yet reviewed” rather than fabricate. This pilot does not call any generative model and keeps the original reading experience free of AI. Any later optional AI explanation requires explicit user opt-in, privacy analysis, factual grounding and a separate approval decision.

## Practical reading paths

- **New to Gurmukhi**: learn how to identify Ang, Raag, Salok, Pauri; read the exact original with optional approximate Roman reading; see an English explanation and a single vocabulary question.
- **Punjabi literate**: original line; close modern Punjabi meaning; grammar; what the previous and following lines do; alternate interpretations when supported.
- **Deep researcher**: word history, commentary citations, contextual cross-references, interpretation caveats, and comparison to a specific other source.
- **Unplanned discovery**: random complete *teaching* unit; retain the option to read the whole enclosing bani immediately.
- **Dedicated Sukhmani study**: select any Ashtpadi 1–24; read full original now. Ashtpadi 17 has an expanded teaching pilot. Never imply all twenty-four have this extra layer merely because all text and existing meaning drafts are already in the app.

## Devices, use and design

Support an iPhone-width primary layout (390 CSS px, and smaller 320/375 px), iPad tablet layouts (768–1024 px), and laptop/desktop (1280–1920 px). The first code slice includes responsive widths at <=720 and <=1100 and a three-column reading desk on wide screens. The text must remain primary; no pop-up comparators covering Gurbani. Requirements:
- persistent full-context source link; 44x44 px minimum relevant controls; scalable Gurmukhi and correct Punjabi language annotation;
- keyboard and screen-reader ordering, semantic headings, focus return, reduced motion and light/dark OS themes;
- optional short summaries and collapsible deep material, accessible without relying on gesture or hover;
- no accounts, purchases, streaks, analytics, unsafe generated recitation, or externally loaded fonts;
- original reader offline and saved learning must remain intact. **The new Explore route is not yet included in the production service worker**, so do not claim offline acceptance for it before release integration.
- pending actual-device tests: iPhone Safari, iPad Safari with touch and split-screen, desktop Safari/Chrome/Firefox, VoiceOver, zoom 200–400%, orientation changes, airplane-mode/offline updates.
