Choose a bani, paste a YouTube link, and the Gurbani will follow the recitation.

  1. Open a Gurbani video and press play.
  2. Tap “First line starts now” when the recitation begins. The light spreads the bani across the video.
  3. If the light drifts, tap the line being recited. It snaps there and learns the pace. For an exact match, use Teach timing once; after that the video always follows.

About

The video plays inside YouTube's own player in privacy-enhanced mode. Nothing is downloaded and nothing listens to the sound. The timing is a small list of "this line starts at this second" marks, kept only on this device unless you share it. The Gurbani shown is the library's own text, checked line by line against its stored fingerprints before it appears.
