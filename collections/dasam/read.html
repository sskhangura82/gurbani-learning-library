About this edition and its review

This reader contains all 67,758 original-text records in the Shabad OS SDGR / DGDG digital edition, with reported pages 1–1428. Its exact original text, punctuation and line order are preserved.

The provider's bibliography uses different printed sources for different compositions, including SGPC and Mehron publications. This is a named digital collection drawn from those sources; it is not presented as one independently collated SGPC printed edition.

Page labels, section names, groups and author attributions follow the provider. A complete source group means the provider's complete ordered group; it does not establish a traditional composition boundary or settle disputed authorship. One group contains page labels that go backwards; those source labels are retained without reordering its lines. The page numbers here are not Ang references in Sri Guru Granth Sahib Ji.

Digital source matching is complete; independent printed collation and editorial review remain pending. This reader displays original text only. Explanations are being developed separately. If any required source file cannot be checked, the reading stays closed. Full offline availability has not been added.
