How this collection will grow

Each additional composition will carry its title, language, selected edition, exact source reference and any recorded textual differences. A source's page numbering belongs to that edition; it will not be treated as an Ang reference in Sri Guru Granth Sahib Ji.

We will keep original text, translation, explanations and historical discussion distinct. Differences between editions and disputed historical attributions will be stated rather than silently resolved.

Sources and current review status

The three links above open the existing reading packages, with their existing edition records, saved place and reflections. This collection page does not replace their text or claim a new textual collation.

For the expansion, the Shabad OS source bibliography identifies composition-specific printed references, including SGPC's Nitnem Te Hor Baniaa(n) and Das Granthi. That bibliography is a research starting point, not a claim that the existing packages have all been checked against those printed copies.

Teaching remains labelled as educational draft material. Source inspection, the two-reviewer editorial check and printed collation are recorded separately.
