About this edition and its review

This reader contains all 60,555 original-text records in the Shabad OS SGGS / SSA2 digital edition, covering its reported Angs 1–1430. The exact spelling, punctuation and line order are preserved. This named edition can differ from the editions used by the library's existing Bani readers.

Ang labels, section names, groups and author attributions follow the provider. A complete source group means the provider's complete ordered group; it does not establish a traditional composition boundary or settle a disputed attribution. Digital source matching is complete; independent printed collation and editorial review remain pending.

Translations and explanations for this full collection are being developed separately. This reader displays original text only. Source files are checked before a reading appears; if a required file is unavailable, the reading stays closed. Full offline availability has not been added.
